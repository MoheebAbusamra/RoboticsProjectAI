## Adaptations

- Workshop pack: selected the CNC lathe, resized and reoriented it, and added industrial paint and metal materials. Other workshop objects were not included.
- Conventional lathe: resized and reduced from 11,968 triangles to approximately 4,300 triangles.
- Conveyor: resized to the existing conveyor footprint; reused the central press assembly in the hazard cell. Optional transparent enclosure panes were omitted to avoid additional transparent rendering passes.
- Racking: reused posts and shelf boards in four bays, fitted to existing occupied rack footprints. Existing inventory and pickup surfaces were retained.
- Workbench: fitted to the existing QC bench footprint and cargo tray height.
- Industrial pack: selected four containers and a process cylinder, placed on existing occupied storage/work surfaces.
- CNC panel: resized, reoriented and reduced to approximately 1,250 triangles.
- All selected geometry: detached from source hierarchies after evaluating transforms; exported with normals and UVs; diffuse color and local ambient occlusion baked into one 2048-pixel JPEG atlas, with three lit material batches. Direct scene lighting remains dynamic.

Existing Northline factory shell, floor finish, signs, robot models, forklift, workers and cargo are separate pre-existing project assets. See ASSET_SOURCES.md for their provenance.
