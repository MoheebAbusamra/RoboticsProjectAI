# Surface sources

- [ambientCG Concrete034](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034), CC0. 2K source, tinted and composited into a 2048px dark polished concrete floor atlas with static contact shading.
- [ambientCG Metal032](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal032), CC0. 1K source, tinted and baked into equipment and robot atlases.
- [License](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/).

Meshes are custom authored in Blender. High-frequency finish and local AO are baked into compact atlases. No high-poly physics meshes. Direct lighting and specular response remain active at runtime.
